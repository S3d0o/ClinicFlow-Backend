## 1. Summary

P1 adds the first `/api/v2` endpoints, so the contract becomes **4.0.0**. In order:
- **The clinic in data:** organization profile, branches with working hours, holidays and rooms, and typed organization settings that replace hard-coded rules (cancellation window, no-show grace).
- **People and access:** staff members with role assignments per branch, so branch scope becomes real in `IAccessGuard`. Also invitations, deactivation, the doctor professional profile, TOTP MFA for staff, and `GET /api/v2/me` with effective permissions.
- **Accounts and messages:** sign-up by Egyptian mobile number with one-time codes by WhatsApp (SMS built but switched off), login by phone or email, preferred language, and AR/EN message templates that replace V1's English emails.
- **Hiring:** file storage with virus scanning, then doctor applications through Applied → Interview → Accepted, with previewed stage emails and activation on the start date. This replaces V1's doctor self-registration and approval.
- **Catalog:** services, price list with most-specific-price resolution, doctor–service mapping; plus the audit viewer.

**Decided by this plan** (no owner input needed; reversible):
- **Staff roles come from assignments.** Role claims in the token come from `StaffRoleAssignment` for staff and from Identity's `Patient` role for patients. Existing `Admin` and `Doctor` Identity role rows are backfilled into staff members and then stop being the source. Inherited `[Authorize(Roles = ...)]` attributes keep working because the claim values are the same role names.
- **Doctor profile visibility.** `DoctorProfile.IsPublic` replaces `IsApprovedByAdmin` in every query, inherited public routes included. The old column is backfilled into it and stays until the import tool no longer needs it (09 §2).
- **`ConsultationFee` stays readable** on inherited routes until P2's public booking shows prices from the price list (FR-SVC-04); P1's backfill copies it into the price list.

- PR 18 comes last.
- Organization (PR 1–3) and catalog (PR 12) can move earlier or later freely.

## 6. Risks

- **Size.** P1 is the largest phase so far (size L, about 19 PRs). Deferring PR 16 and PR 17 (Q-C, Q-D) removes two.
- **Authentication is rewritten while inherited routes still serve the frontend.**
  - Tokens, login, MFA and phone identity change in PRs 4, 8 and 10.
  - Every PR keeps the inherited auth routes working until PR 18 deletes them.
  - The existing auth integration tests stay green as the safety net.
- **Staff can lock themselves out.** Recovery codes, the Admin reset and the bootstrap admin (`Seeding:AdminEmail`) are the way back. The bootstrap admin's first login must allow enrolment.
- **The frontend is building P1 screens now** against fake services (§7). Shapes are agreed in the contract before each PR (Q-A), and the mirror goes out with each contract change, as in P0.
- **Phone channels:**
  - Meta business verification and WhatsApp template approval can take weeks (Q2), and so can SMS sender-name registration if SMS is switched on. Start them at P1's kickoff. P1 itself isn't blocked: it is built and tested against the log channel.
  - Some patients have no WhatsApp. Until SMS is switched on, they can't receive a code (`Messaging.PhoneUnreachable`), so the share of unreachable numbers is watched in staging before deciding whether SMS goes live at launch.
  - Cost abuse is handled by the caps, the rate limit and the spend alert. CAPTCHA is held in reserve.
- **ClamAV** needs about 1 GB of RAM and a signature volume.
  - Its compose service is optional for development (the fake scanner covers tests).
  - On this machine C: is nearly full, so its image and volume should live with Docker's disk on K: (move Docker's disk image first).
- **Phone uniqueness backfill** (PR 10) can fail on existing data; the pre-check lists offenders first.
